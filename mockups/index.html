What changed from the current UI

Removed or moved

  • The marketing hero on the Inbox. The first screen is now work.
  • Star field, nova glows and spotlight cards. Decorative motion is cut to one element per screen.
  • Sponsor chips in the inbox and footer. They belong on an About or Evals page.
  • Six top-level tabs. Reduced to Today, Inbound, Sites, Data and Playbook, with Evals tucked below.
  • Radar as a separate page. It now sits on the home screen in B (sized by money at risk), and A and C show days open inline.

Added

  • One "needs you" count at the top of every layout.
  • Sites sorted by priority, with the next step written out.
  • A "where this lives" view: mailbox, sandbox (deleted), Claude extraction, Postgres and S3, with what is kept and what is not.
  • Document versions per site, newest first.
  • Status always as text plus color, never color alone.
